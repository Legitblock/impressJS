🌲

RFC 6962 Domain Merkle Trees

Domain-separated prefixes (0x00 leaf, 0x01 branch) prevent length extension and second-preimage collision attacks during independent clause verification.

H_leaf = sha256(0x00 || sha256(clause)) H_branch = sha256(0x01 || left || right)
🛡️

Pedersen Commitments

Homomorphic commitments C = g^v * h^r mod P provide information-theoretic privacy for financial metrics while remaining computationally binding.

Commitment: C = g^value * h^blinding mod P Homomorphic: C(a + b) = C(a) * C(b) mod P
